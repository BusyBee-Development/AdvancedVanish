## Before asking

1. Check the [FAQ](faq.md), [Common Issues](common-issues.md) and
   [Troubleshooting](../advanced-optimization/troubleshooting.md).
2. Make sure you're on the latest release from
   [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/BusyBee-Development/AdvancedVanish/releases).

## Where to ask

- **Bugs and feature requests:** [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/BusyBee-Development/AdvancedVanish/issues)
- **Questions and help:** [Discord](https://discord.gg/abdm29q7af)

## What to include

- The output of `/version` and `/vanish version`.
- Whether you run Paper or Folia.
- The AdvancedVanish startup block from the console (the five lines starting with
  `AdvancedVanish v...`).
- Any error or warning from the console, in full.
- Your `config.yml`, with passwords removed.
- What you did, what you expected, and what happened instead.
